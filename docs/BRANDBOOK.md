## 1. Positioning

**Category:** Smart electrical apparel — a new category, not a wearable accessory.

**One-line positioning**
> VOLTWEAR is the first garment that reads the body's native electrical language — heart, muscle
> and spine — and turns it into one instruction you can act on today.

**Positioning statement**
> For serious athletes and the chronically seated who have outgrown wrist-based wearables,
> VOLTWEAR is a smart electrical garment that measures the body at the source — clinical ECG,
> 16-channel EMG and a nine-node spinal array across 1,240 cm² of torso — because effort,
> fatigue and injury begin in muscle and spine, not in a pulse wave at the wrist.

**Three pillars**
| Pillar | Claim | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| **Source truth** | We measure where it happens | 1,240 cm² contact · 31 nodes · 1 kHz EMG |
| **Prescription** | We tell you what to do | VOLT-1 on-device · 0.83 AUC injury model |
| **Disappearance** | You forget you're wearing it | 132 g · 1.9 mm laminate · 60 machine washes |

**What we are not:** a fitness tracker, a coaching app, a medical device (yet), a fashion brand.

## 2. Naming

### 2.1 Assessment of "VOLTWEAR"
**Premium score: 6.4 / 10**

*Strengths.* "Volt" is the most ownable single word available in this category: short, hard
consonants, universally understood, and it names the actual differentiator (electrical sensing
rather than optical inference).

*Weaknesses.* The `-wear` suffix caps the company at apparel and is the most crowded suffix in
consumer goods. It reads as a product description rather than a company. Neuralink is not
"BrainWear"; WHOOP is not "StrapWear". When the roadmap reaches VOLT OS and a licensing business,
the name actively works against the story.

### 2.2 Five stronger alternatives

| # | Name | Meaning | Rationale | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **AXON** | The nerve fibre that carries an electrical impulse away from the cell body | Names the mechanism, not the product. Four letters, hard opening consonant, nothing to outgrow. Sits beside Oura and Neuralink without explanation. Makes the technology sound inevitable rather than novel. | **9.5** |
| 2 | **VOLTA** | Alessandro Volta, inventor of the battery and origin of the word *volt* | Retains every bit of equity in "Volt" while shedding the apparel ceiling. The terminal *A* adds Italian warmth to a hard technical root — the Ferrari/Prada move. Easiest migration: same first four letters. | **9.0** |
| 3 | **OHM** | The unit of electrical resistance — and the sound of stillness | A rare double meaning spanning both halves of the product: instrument and recovery. Three letters, unmispronounceable in any market. The most Oura-adjacent in feel. | **8.8** |
| 4 | **KORE** | The core — the trunk, the exact territory this garment owns | Direct and physical. The K spelling makes it registrable and gives it an engineered edge. Extends to a family: Kore One, Kore Pro, Kore Clinic. | **8.6** |
| 5 | **MYOS** | From *myo-*, Greek for muscle, as in myoelectric | The most technically precise option — myoelectric signal is literally what the shirt reads. Clinical enough for the medical channel. Needs one line of explanation on first contact, which is the only reason it ranks fifth. | **8.2** |

*Scoring weights: phonetic hardness 20% · syllable economy 15% · category headroom 25% ·
trademark viability 20% · how it reads engraved on a $1,290 aluminium case 20%.*

### 2.3 Recommendation
**Ship as VOLTWEAR. Secure AXON.**
41,300 pre-orders and all press coverage are already attached to VOLTWEAR, and it is genuinely the
better *product* name. Acquire and hold AXON now, and execute the platform rebrand alongside the
VOLT OS licensing launch. This entire identity system is built so that swapping the wordmark
breaks nothing — the symbol, palette, type and layout system are all name-independent.

## 3. Logo

### 3.1 The Arc — symbol mark
A **V** built from two tapered strokes with a signal bolt descending through it. It reads
simultaneously as: the letter V, a garment neckline, a voltage vector, and a signal travelling
down the body.

- Constructed on a **32-unit grid**, stroke weight **3 units**, terminals rounded at 1.5 units.
- Bolt stroke is **1.9 units** — always lighter than the V, always Platinum or the ground colour.
- Angle of the V: **±26° from vertical**. Never adjusted.

### 3.2 Lockups
| Lockup | Use |
|---|---|
| Symbol only | App icon, favicon, garment chest, hub engraving, social avatar |
| Horizontal (symbol + wordmark, 0.5× gap) | Website nav, documents, email, packaging |
| Stacked (symbol above wordmark) | Square formats, booth signage, hang tags |
| Wordmark only | Footer, legal, embroidered hem tab under 12 mm |

### 3.3 Rules
- **Clear space:** 1× symbol height on all sides. Nothing enters it — ever.
- **Minimum size:** 16 px symbol digital · 84 px horizontal lockup · 18 mm embroidered.
- **Colour:** gradient version (Volt → Mint → Arc) on dark only. Solid Platinum, solid Obsidian,
  or solid Volt everywhere else.
- **Never:** rotate, outline, add effects, gradient the wordmark, stretch, place on photography
  without a scrim, or reproduce in any colour outside the palette.

### 3.4 Explorations considered
| Direction | Why it lost |
|---|---|
| **B · The Weave** — interlocking chevrons | Beautiful at large sizes, illegible below 24 px |
| **C · Pulse Core** — module + QRS complex | Indistinguishable from a dozen shipping health apps |
| **D · Meridian** — V built from sensor nodes | Conceptually the most accurate, first thing to vanish when embroidered |

| **Display** | Suisse Int'l Bold | Inter Tight 600 | 34–148 px · tracking −3.5% to −5% · never <500 weight · never italic |
| **Body** | Inter (variable 300–600) | — | 12.5–21 px · line-height 1.55–1.65 · max 66 characters |
| **Data / UI** | JetBrains Mono 400–500 | IBM Plex Mono | 9–13 px · UPPERCASE · tracking +0.12 to +0.24 em · labels and units only |

**Scale.** 148 / 84 / 54 / 34 / 21 / 15.5 / 13.5 / 11.
**Rule of one.** One display-scale headline per page. If a page needs two, it needs two pages.
**Numbers.** Always tabular figures. A metric is never set in the body face.

## 6. Voice

VOLTWEAR states facts and consequences. It never hypes, never uses an exclamation mark, and never
congratulates the user for existing. The tone that earns a cardiologist's trust is the same tone
that earns an athlete's respect.

| We say | We never say |
|---|---|
| "Your left erector is 9% below baseline." | "Crush your goals! 💪" |
| "Rest. The data says so." | "You're on fire today!" |
| "FDA 510(k) pending for ECG indications." | "Medical-grade!" (unqualified) |
| "1,240 cm² of contact." | "Revolutionary breakthrough technology." |

**Five principles**
1. Number first, adjective never.
2. Every claim carries its evidence or its caveat.
3. Prescribe, don't describe.
4. Short sentences. Engineering cadence.
5. Respect the body. It is not a machine to be hacked.

## 7. Motion

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Standard easing | `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)` |
| Standard duration | 340 ms UI · 900 ms scroll reveal |
| Live data | Spring, no fixed duration — data must never look animated |
| Signal flow | Trace pulses travel at a constant 62 px/s regardless of scale |
| Reduce-motion | Fully honoured. No animation carries information. |

**Principle.** Motion in this brand always represents *signal travelling through a conductor*.
Nothing bounces, nothing spins for delight, nothing eases in from off-screen without a reason.

## 8. Photography & imagery

- **Effort, never joy.** No smiling, no eye contact, no group celebration shots.
- **One light source.** If you can identify two key lights, relight it.
- **Sweat is mandatory** in any motion image.
- **The garment is never the subject of a lifestyle shot** — the body is, and the garment is on it.
- **Never** stock gyms, sunrise typography, chalk clouds in slow motion, or a hand holding a phone.
