## 2. Campaign

### Master line
> ## Read the signal.

Three words. Imperative mood. Simultaneously an instruction to the customer and a description of
what the product does. Translates without losing the double meaning; survives being shouted across
a trade-show floor.

### Line system
| Line | Use | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Read the signal.** | Master | See above |
| **The body has always been electric.** | Brand film, hero | Reframes the category — not a new gadget, the first correct reading of something always happening |
| **Wrists guess. Fabric knows.** | Competitive media | The sharpest thing we can say without naming a competitor. Runs where WHOOP and Apple already spend |
| **1,240 cm² of truth.** | Technical, paid social | A number nobody can argue with and no wristband can match |
| **Your form, before it breaks.** | Injury prevention, clinical | Sells the outcome that makes people pay: fewer injuries, not more data |
| **Wear the instrument.** | Retail, closing | Frames the garment as laboratory equipment — the premium the price requires |

### Three concepts

**01 · THE TRACE** — *Read the signal.*
A single athlete in near-darkness. The only light in frame is the conductive trace network catching
a rim from one side. The traces continue off the body and become the ECG line running across the
bottom of the frame into the logo. 85 mm, ISO pushed, grain intact.
→ Brand film 60/30/15 · cinema + CTV · hero OOH.

**02 · WRISTS GUESS** — *Wrists guess. Fabric knows.*
Split frame. Left: a wrist, a single number, deliberately flat and grey. Right: the same moment
rendered from the chest — sixteen muscle channels, an ECG complex, a spine. No voiceover. The
comparison does the work in under four seconds.
→ Paid social 6s + 15s · YouTube pre-roll · retail endcap.

**03 · SIX DAYS** — *Your form, before it breaks.*
A hamstring tear, in reverse. The film runs backwards from the injury to six days earlier, where
the risk flag is already amber on a screen nobody looked at. Ends on the app, the flag, the line.
The only emotional film in the campaign — it earns the clinical tone of everything else.
→ Brand film 90s · team and federation sales · press launch.

## 5. Social system

Every organic post is a real screenshot of real telemetry with one line of copy. No lifestyle
photography, no stock gyms, no motivational typography over a sunrise. The feed should look like a
lab notebook that happens to be beautiful.

| Post | Visual | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1/4 · ECG | Live 5-lead trace | This is a heartbeat. Not an estimate of one. |
| 2/4 · EMG | 16-channel activation bars | Which muscle actually did the rep? |
| 3/4 · Posture | Spine reconstruction, +9.4° | Every 15° forward is 12 kg on your neck. |
| 4/4 · AI | Terminal-style risk readout | It flagged the tear six days early. |

**Cadence:** 4 organic posts/week, 1 long-form technical thread/week, 1 athlete data story/month.

## 6. Packaging

Primary carton 340 × 240 × 62 mm, FSC E-flute, 100% recycled, **zero plastic**.
Soft-touch matte black, Volt hot-foil symbol, blind-deboss trace field.

**Unboxing sequence**
1. Sleeve — soft-touch black, Volt foil mark
2. Lid — magnetic close, 1.2 s damped opening
3. Garment — folded to show the chest trace array, on a felt cradle
4. Calibration certificate — uncoated 320 gsm, serial + technician initials
5. Lower tray — VoltCore hub in anodised aluminium, magnetically retained
6. Braided USB-C, 0.4 m, Volt-tipped

The garment is the only thing visible for the first four seconds. The electronics reveal themselves
underneath, in sequence, like a specimen case.

## 10. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Smart apparel's history of failure (Athos, Hexoskin) | Motion-artefact cancellation is the reason they failed and the reason we don't. Lead every technical conversation with it. |
| FDA timeline slips | Every claim shipping at launch is wellness-exempt. ECG is upside, not dependency. |
| Wash durability doubt | 60-cycle guarantee, in writing, with free replacement. Publish the drift data. |
| Apple enters the category | They cannot ship apparel at scale in nine sizes with a 14-month replacement cycle. Our moat is textile manufacturing, not silicon. |
| Sizing returns | Free first exchange; app-based 14-point fit scan before shipment. |
