05 · Trade show

CES 2027 · Booth 21514

A 12 × 9 m island stand built around a single idea: the visitor becomes the demo. You put the shirt on at the perimeter, walk the loop, and your own signal renders on the 6 m LED volume in the centre while you move.

Plan · 12 × 9 m islandEXH-PLAN
Elevation · LED volume + demo baysEXH-ELEV
Zone A
The Fitting

Six private carbon-fibre changing pods on the perimeter. Ninety seconds to get the garment on and paired. Staff never touch the visitor.

Zone B
The Volume

A 6 m cubic LED volume. Your live ECG, muscle activation and spine render at architectural scale while you move through it. One person at a time.

Zone C
The Teardown

The exploded garment in a 3 m acrylic vitrine, backlit, with a working microscope on the electrode micro-pillars. For the engineers and the press.

Zone D
The Room

Eight-seat closed meeting room behind the volume. Acoustic felt, one table, no screens except the one on the wall. Where the enterprise deals actually get done.
