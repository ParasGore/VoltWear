The thesis

Wrists measure
the aftermath.
Fabric measures
the event.

Every wearable on earth today is clamped to a wrist or a finger — the two places furthest from the muscles doing the work. They infer. They lag. They guess.

VOLTWEAR sits on 1,240 cm² of torso skin: over the heart, over the spine, over the prime movers. It doesn't infer effort from a pulse wave — it reads the motor unit recruitment that caused it, 1,000 times a second, before the movement completes.

Technology

Six layers.
Two millimetres.

The entire sensing platform lives inside a 1.9 mm laminate — thinner than a credit card, stretchable to 180%, and washable 60 times without signal degradation.

  • Outer shell — AeroKnit™ 4-way
    78% recycled Italian nylon 6.6 / 22% Lycra® Sport. 118 gsm. UPF 40. Laser-perforated in 11 mapped ventilation zones.
    0.42 mm
  • Silver-yarn signal bus
    Elastomeric conductive yarn (silver-plated nylon, 12 Ω/m) knitted as a serpentine so it stretches 180% without resistance drift.
    0.19 mm
  • TPU dielectric isolation film
    Thermally bonded polyurethane. Isolates the bus from skin and sweat, IPX7 sealed, prevents crosstalk between EMG channels.
    0.06 mm
  • Sensor plane — 31 nodes
    16 EMG · 5 ECG · 6 IMU · 2 thermistor · 2 respiration. Each on a flexible polyimide island with strain-relief spirals.
    0.71 mm
  • Hydrogel-free dry electrodes
    Ag/AgCl-loaded silicone micro-pillar array. Contact impedance <12 kΩ dry, and it improves as you sweat.
    0.34 mm
  • Next-to-skin CoolTouch liner
    Jade-mineral micro-modal, antimicrobial Polygiene®, flatlock bonded — zero seams over any sensor island.
    0.18 mm
VoltCore™ edge compute

A 6-gram titanium-shell module magnetically docks at the left rib. Inside: a 0.9 TOPS NPU running the coaching model on-device, 512 MB of buffer for offline training, and a 210 mAh silicon-anode cell rated for 9 days.

Motion-artefact cancellation

The problem that killed every smart shirt before us. Six IMUs give us a mechanical model of the fabric itself, so we subtract the garment's motion from the biosignal — adaptive LMS filtering at 0.4 ms latency.

Capabilities

Eight systems.
One garment.

Each subsystem was built to be best-in-class as a standalone product. Together they compound into something no single-point sensor can approach.

Clinical ECG

Five-lead morphology at 512 Hz — the same trace a cardiologist reads. Continuous AFib detection, QT interval, ectopic beat classification.

16-channel EMG

Motor-unit recruitment per muscle group. See exactly which muscle fired, how hard, how fast, and whether your left matched your right.

Spinal telemetry

Nine IMUs from C7 to L3 reconstruct your spine 200×/sec. Real-time haptic correction the instant your form breaks under load.

Recovery & readiness

Overnight HRV, respiratory rate, skin-temp delta and sleep architecture resolve into one number you can actually act on before 7 a.m.

AI coach

VOLT-1 speaks in your ear mid-set: "drop 5 kg, your left erector is compensating." Prescriptive, not descriptive.

Thermal & hydration

Dual thermistors model core temperature within 0.19 °C and flag dehydration from sweat conductivity before performance drops.
