01 · Dashboard
One number, then permission

Recovery owns the top third of the screen because it is the only number that changes today's behaviour. Everything below it exists to justify that number — HRV, RHR, sleep architecture, respiratory rate. Tap the ring and it inverts into the contributing factors.

Primary actionAccept today's plan
Glance time1.4 s
RefreshOn wake, 05:00 local
02 · Workout
Legible at 160 bpm

Designed to be read at arm's length, mid-set, with sweat on the screen. Type never drops below 15 pt, contrast never below 7:1, and the muscle map uses colour and area together so it survives a glance. The ECG strip is real — it draws at the sample rate.

Primary actionNone — passive
Latency budget120 ms sensor → pixel
Also renders onWatch, TV, sideline tablet
03 · Posture
A body, not a bar chart

Posture data is spatial, so the visualisation is spatial. The spine is reconstructed from nine IMUs and rendered against its own neutral baseline — you see your deviation as a shape, in degrees, in the place it is happening.

Primary actionStart a 90-second reset
InterventionHaptic first, screen second
CadenceContinuous, silent
04 · AI Coach
Prescriptive, never descriptive

VOLT-1 does not tell you what happened — it tells you what to do, and shows the evidence underneath in a collapsed drawer. Every recommendation carries a confidence and a "why". You can argue with it, and it updates.

Primary actionApply to today
